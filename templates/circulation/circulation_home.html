🔄 Circulation Information
  • Select a member and an available book copy to issue a book.
  • The due date is calculated automatically according to the member's membership type.
  • When a book is returned after the due date, the applicable fine is calculated automatically.
  • Book copy status changes automatically between Available, Issued and Lost.
  • Use Overdue Books to find books that have passed their due date.
